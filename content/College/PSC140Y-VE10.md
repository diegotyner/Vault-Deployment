2010 census:
- 2.7 mil grandparents responsible for basic needs of grandkids
- 1/3 households headed by a gradmother/father or both (39.8 mil)
	- Of these, 4.5 mil contain one or more grandchildren
	- Of these, 1/3 (1.5 mil) don't contain bio parent of grandkid
		- "Skipped generation households"

Difficulties:
- Grandparents may not be emotionally, financially, or physically prepared for full time case of grandparents
- Also, generational gap may be an issue

Generally arised in negative ways that make grandparents intervene, that can cause trauma for kids:
- Drugs, abuse, poverty, neglect, ...
