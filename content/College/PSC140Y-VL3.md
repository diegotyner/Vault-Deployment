All of the aforemention systems interact and influence each other

	- affects institutions like the media which shape discourse and policies on the exosystem level
	- influences individual experiences on a microsystem (e.g. discussions about race)

##### Through language development
Microsystem - Parents and direct environment are the primary source of language input
Mesosystem - (school env and healthcare for example) can intersect and have a role in language development and create special accomodations.
Exosystem - Availability of community resources can provide opportunities for the child to engage in language enhancing experiences
Macrosystem - Cultures have different expectations about: forms of language and how much the child should be talked to (some cultures baby talk to their infants while in other they only overhear parents conversations)
Chronosystem - Social media significantly changed language

##### Recap
- Ecological Systems highlights contextual factors in understanding human development
- Child development as a complex system of relationships affected by multiple levels of environments
- Implications for educational practice and understanding diverse contexts
