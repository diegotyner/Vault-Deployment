Cognition - Mental activities of thinking, knowing, remembering, communicating

Jean Piaget (1896-1980):
- Stages of development, children are not miniature adults and think fundamentally different
- Schemas - representations of the owrl
1. Sensorimotor (0-2yrs)
2. Preoperational (2-7yrs)
3. Concrete operational (7-11yrs) - "true logical thought"
4. Formal operational (11+yrs)
