### Theory of Mind
Before age 3, children have problem with false belief tasks

Theory of mind

Children from large families perform better on false belief tasks

Before theory of mind:
- Poor performance on certain tasks
- Say mom would believe candy was in crayon box because that's what they believe
After:
- Correctly say mom would believe crayons to be in box

Theory of mind also needed to perform tricks/deceive others

---

### Developing a Theory of Mind
Age 2 or 3: Good thinkers, but don't consider their own thinking or others
Around age 4: begin to develop a theory of mind

*False-belief problems* - setting up a situation in which a child believes incorrectly that another person shares the same knowledge that the child has 
- Crayon box problem
- Three mountains task
- Sally and Annie ball task ("where will Sally look for her ball?")
*Egocentrism* - Centered on their own perspective
