### Probability Recap
$$
\begin{align*}
\text{Conditional Probability: } P(x|y) = \frac{P(x,y)}{P(y)} \\
\text{Product Rule: } P(x,y) = P(x|y)P(y)\\
\text{Chain Rule: } P(X_1, \dots, X_n) = P(X_1)P(X_2|X_1)P(X_3|X_1,X_2) \\
= \Pi^n_{i=1} P(X_i|X_1, \dots X_i) \\
\text{X,Y independent iff: } \forall x,y : P(x,y) = P(x)P(y) \\
\text{X,Y conditionally indepedendent given Z iff: } \forall x, y, z : P(x,y|z) = P(x|z)P(y|z)
\end{align*}
$$
