## Continued

Outcomes:
*Neglect* - Tend to be sad, lonely, and immaturate for their age. AS they age, tend to have trouble maintaining relationships and are at higher risk for substance use
*Authoritarian* - Children are obedient but unhappy and unfriendly. Low self-confidence and perform poorly at school, high risk for aggressive behavior. Can't wait to leave home
*Permissive* - Most likely to be impulsive and lack self-control or regulation. As young adults, tend to become independent at a slower pace
*Authoritative* - Likely to be competent and successful, happy with themselves, popular with peers, generous toward others. At low risk of developing substance use disorder and other behavioral issues.

### Bidirectionality
Parents behavior is influenced by child behavior
Many parents would prefer an authoritarian style, but angry children challenging every rule may push parents toward an authoritarian style
