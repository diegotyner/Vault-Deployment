> Before we begin, briefly explain what you already know about how nature and nurture shape development?
```
Both nature and nurture have enormous roles in human development.
- Nature: The part of development that is predetermined: genetics.
- Nurture: The part of development that is not predetermined, but molded by upbringing: environment.
```
