### Continuity and Discontinuity

#### How is early dev related to later dev?
How are chars at one point in time relted to your chars at a later point in time
Is the dev *gradual and continuous* or does it happen in *stages*

##### Continuous 
If you can count it (height, weight, words in vocab, cells in the body)
	- When the same behavior is seen at different ages, just different amount/speeds
##### Discontinuous
Different structures and behaviors suggest discontinuous dev
- Like stages of a butterfly (caterpilar, chrysalis, butterfly)
- Child producing first word (discontinuity, preverbal to verbal) or learning to read

#### Stages
*Conception* - Sperm fertilizing egg, 1 cell. 
*Birth* - Body has billions of cells, a hundred billion in the brain. 

A discontinuity between the two stages of development. Not only increasing cell number, but new structures are present.

#### 3 distinct stages of prenatal development
Distinct from trimesters, those are descriptive of time not quality
##### Zygote (germinal stage)
1st weeks after conception (conception - 3 weeks)
Zygote (created at conception by sperm+egg) divides into multiple cells, diferent types of cells, and becomes implanted in uterine wall
##### Embryonic stage
3-8 weeks
Begins to develop basic structures (arm, face, etc.)
##### Fetus
8 weeks to birth
Organs grow and develop, eventually become able to function
At the end of period, fetus puts on fat and is born

#### Continuous elements of prenatal development
1. Genes
2. Behavior - Swallowing, breathing, etc.
