### Intelligence
Intelligence:
- Ability to: Reason, apply knowledge, understand

What is it?
- One thing, a few, many:

Intelligence as a single trait:
- G - general intelligence
- Correlation between intelligent traits between people

Intelligence as a few:
- Crystallized intelligence: factual knowledge
	- Increases steadily
- Fluid inteligence: ability to think on the spot
	- Declines with age

Many things:
- Remembering, planning, perceiving, etc

Three stratum:
1) Extremely general - General intelligence
2) Moderately general - fluid, crystallized, learning
3) Specific 

Measuring intelligence (Binet)
- Developed IQ to tell which kids need attention
- Intelligence Quotient
