## 🔶 Course Description
Instruction for students in all disciplines in advanced principles of academic and professional writing. Focus on writing tasks both within and beyond the academy.

This course is designed to help you further develop your academic writing knowledge and skills. Throughout this class, you will become aware of the conventions that writers in your academic field observe in order to help you better understand the ways that these discourse communities make meaning with the written language.
