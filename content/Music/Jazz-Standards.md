Here's where I'm going to put down links helpful for learning jazz standards.

[learnjazzstandards.com](https://www.learnjazzstandards.com/jazz-standards-list/)
- Has list of standards, as well as guides on them and playlists of different renditions
- [Biggest jazz musicians guide](https://www.learnjazzstandards.com/blog/famous-jazz-musicians/)

[the real book search website](https://therealbook.info/)
- Search for lead sheets of standards

[100 must know jazz tunes](https://www.neffmusic.com/blog/2017/05/100-must-know-tune-consensus-list/)
- Consensus list of standards

[internet archive jazz documentary](https://archive.org/details/jazz-season-1-episode-2-the-gift-1917-1924)
- ken burns jazz documentary
