### Review expanding
Fm7
He really loves his **Kenny Baron Voicing**.
- "Stack fifths on left hand, half step up then another stack of fifths"
	- So for a Fm7 with a Bb in the melody: F / C / G / Ab / Eb / Bb
	- This gives a Fm11
	- With smaller hands you can opt for a F/Ab/C/Eb/G/Bb

Ebmaj7
Voicing for major chords when 7th is in melody:
- Start with root, visualize the third, then build a **So What chord** from there
	- Eb / G / C / F / Bb / D
	- 1st / 3rd / 13 / 9 / 5 / maj7
	- Stack of fourths, dreamy sound

Fm7
**Herby Hancock Voicing**
Fm7 and G
- 1 / 5 in LH (F C)
- 3b / 11 / 7b / 9 in RH (Ab / Bb / Eb / G)
- F / C / Ab / Bb / Eb / G
