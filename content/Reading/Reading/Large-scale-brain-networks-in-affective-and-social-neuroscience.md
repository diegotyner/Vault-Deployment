### Scratch Notes:
#### Abstract
This source attempts to tie together the fields of large scale brain networks and social neuroscience. Large scale brain networks being the systematic coactivations of different brain areas and social neuroscience being the study of emotional, social, and cognitive phenomena through the lens of neuroscience. The authors argue that tying them together is a natural next step that can help to advance social neuroscience.

#### Intro
Brain Imaging Goals:
- Understand responses of neurons in mental terms
- Search for the correspondence between psychological faculties and topographically distinct swaths of brain tissues
Assumption of modularity that this approach takes has been increasingly challenged
- Experiments show *patterns* of results that are more similar than a faculty psych approach would suggest
