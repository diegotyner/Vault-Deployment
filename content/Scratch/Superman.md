- solid movie! Pleasantly surprised
- beginning felt weak, shoe on head dialog ("how did you know what he was going to do? Its almost like...", as if the numbers wasnt good enough)
- fantastic job with characters. I got everyone, even the small/new ones. Everyone had motivations, characterization, plot relevance. Very impressive for number of characters and time spent on them. It felt natural too.
- he murdered the shit out of his clone, even though he saved the squirrel. Would have loved an extra scene of superman finding out the clone was braindead or something before talking straight to luthor on radio through him.
- I was a sucker for krypto
- they chickened out of any hard questions by making everyone cartoonishly evil. After the interview scene with lois particularly, questions hang and feel unanswered. Should he be doing this (boravia)? Should there be checks? He's good and we know this, but how do we reconcile the beliefs?
